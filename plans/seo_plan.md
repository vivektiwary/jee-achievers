## 6. Technical SEO

Do these checks after every major update:

- Submit `sitemap.xml` in Google Search Console.
- Inspect the homepage and new SEO pages in Google Search Console.
- Request indexing for:
  - homepage
  - `/teacher-in-marathahalli/`
  - `/best-teacher-spice-garden-bangalore/`
  - `/iit-jee-maths-coaching-marathahalli/`
- Keep page titles under roughly 60 characters when possible.
- Keep meta descriptions under roughly 160 characters when possible.
- Make sure every page has exactly one H1.
- Make sure every SEO page has canonical URL.
- Check that JSON-LD structured data is valid.
- Keep images compressed and use descriptive alt text.
- Avoid large unused scripts.
- Keep mobile page speed high.

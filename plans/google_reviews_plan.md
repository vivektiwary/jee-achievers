# Google Reviews Collection Plan

Goal: collect authentic Google reviews that improve trust, local SEO, and conversion for JEEPinnacle.

## Why Google Reviews Matter

Google reviews help with:

- local map visibility
- searches like "teacher in Marathahalli" and "best teacher near Spice Garden"
- parent trust before they call
- proof that the coaching is real and active
- conversion from website visitors to demo bookings

For this business, reviews should naturally mention:

- Gunjan Sir
- Maths teacher
- Science teacher
- Class 12 PCM
- JEE Maths
- Marathahalli
- Spice Garden
- doubt clearing
- concept clarity
- parent updates
- small batches

Do not ask people to write fake or forced keywords. Give prompts that help them describe their real experience.

## Step 4: Who To Ask First

Start with people most likely to write genuine, specific reviews.

Priority order:

1. Parents of current students who are happy with consistency or progress
2. Students who improved in Maths confidence or marks
3. Parents who joined because of personal attention
4. Past students who cleared JEE, KCET, COMEDK, BITSAT, VITEEE or board exams
5. Students who moved from weak basics to problem-solving confidence

Initial target:

- first 10 reviews within 30 days
- 25 reviews within 3 months
- 50 reviews within 6-9 months

## Step 5: When To Ask

Ask when the parent/student has just experienced value.

Best moments:

- after a good demo class
- after a student solves a difficult topic
- after improvement in a test
- after a parent says they are happy
- after completing one month of classes
- after exam results
- after a student gets admission or clears an exam

Avoid asking:

- before the student has experienced enough classes
- during a complaint or scheduling issue
- immediately after payment
- in a pushy way

## Step 7: Review Prompt To Share

Some people want to help but do not know what to write. Send this prompt:

You can write about:

- Which class or exam you prepared for
- How Gunjan Sir helped
- Whether concepts became clearer
- Whether doubts were solved properly
- Whether practice became more consistent
- Whether the classes helped for boards, JEE, KCET, COMEDK, BITSAT or VITEEE
- Your area, if comfortable, such as Marathahalli, Spice Garden or Brookefield

Example structure:

> My child studied [subject/exam] with Gunjan Sir. The classes helped with [concept clarity/doubt solving/practice/marks/confidence]. We liked [personal attention/regular updates/small batch approach]. Recommended for students looking for [Maths/Science/JEE/Class 12] support.

Do not ask them to copy this exactly. It should only help them write their real experience.

## Step 8: What Not To Do

Avoid anything that can damage trust or violate platform expectations.

- Do not buy reviews.
- Do not write reviews from fake accounts.
- Do not ask people to use exact same text.
- Do not pressure unhappy parents.
- Do not offer discounts or rewards in exchange for reviews.
- Do not remove or hide all negative feedback; respond calmly and professionally.
- Do not keyword-stuff review requests.

## Step 9: Respond To Every Review

Reply to every review within 24-48 hours.

Response style:

- mention student/parent only if they already made it public
- thank them personally
- mention the teaching value naturally
- keep it short
- do not reveal private results or personal details

### Positive Review Response

Thank you for the kind words. We are glad the classes helped with concept clarity and confidence. Gunjan Sir focuses on personal attention, regular doubt clearing, and steady practice so students can build a strong foundation.

### Parent Review Response

Thank you for trusting JEEPinnacle. We are happy to support [student/class if public] with consistent practice, doubt clearing, and parent updates. Feedback like this helps other families understand our teaching approach.

### Result-Oriented Review Response

Thank you for sharing this. It is great to see the progress. We will continue focusing on concept clarity, problem solving, and exam-oriented practice.

### Negative Or Mixed Review Response

Thank you for sharing your feedback. We are sorry the experience did not fully meet expectations. Please contact us directly at +91 83109 06345 so we can understand the issue and improve.
